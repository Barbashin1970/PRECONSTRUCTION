# Worked pre-sales studies (reference examples)

Three **complete** reports produced by this skill on real topics with web research and an audit.
Use them as a **reference for structure and the quality bar** before you write your own report
(Phase 3): how deep it should be, how sources and vendor-claims are tagged, what the "AI
applicability" section with a verdict, the "Blocking questions (go/no-go)" and the audit log look like.

| Example | Task type | What it demonstrates |
|---------|-----------|----------------------|
| [ai-construction-emerging-markets.md](ai-construction-emerging-markets.md) | Market study (macro, several countries) | TAM/SAM/SOM + source spread, vendor-claim discounting, bottom-up as an unresolved go/no-go |
| [smartcity-koltsovo-novosibirsk.md](smartcity-koltsovo-novosibirsk.md) | Local "why isn't it being built" case | problem reframing, forgotten competitors/instruments, go/no-go blockers |
| [novosibirsk-dirt-lawns-curbs.md](novosibirsk-dirt-lawns-curbs.md) | Infrastructure/policy program + cost estimate | validating AND correcting the client's hypothesis, sanity-check, incumbent ("occupied field"), NO-GO verdict, AI not in the core + layers |

> These are **frozen teaching references**, not fresh data. Their figures are as of the study
> date (2026-06-10) and need re-verification for real use. The skill writes new reports into the
> project's `research/` folder.

**How to use while working:** before consolidating (Phase 3), skim the one example closest to
your task type and keep its structure/bar as a guide. Don't copy the content — copy the level of
rigor (sources, honesty of the AI verdict, explicit blockers).
