# SmartCity / "Integrad" near Koltsovo along the Eastern Bypass (Novosibirsk): why it isn't being built yet

> Presale research. Consolidated from 4 streams (business analysis, marketing/infrastructure, technical expertise, AI applicability) and verified by cross-audit. The text uses **only audit-confirmed material**; everything unconfirmed is explicitly flagged as a **"hypothesis"** or **"needs verification"**.

---

## Executive summary

- **The project is neither hypothetical nor "abandoned" — it is legally launched, but it is stalling at the point where developers should be entering.** Between Akademgorodok and the science town of Koltsovo, the "SmartCity-Novosibirsk" project is being implemented (rebranded → "Integrad" in 2025). Resolution of the Novosibirsk Oblast (NSO) government — March 2023, master plan approved on 4 February 2025, operator — JSC "ARZhS NSO" (Housing Construction Development Agency). The correct framing of the question is: "why is a launched project failing to attract developers and demand," not "why has it not been started."
- **Key fact-marker (confirmed by all streams): the land-plot tenders failed — NOT A SINGLE bid.** 5 lots, starting price RUB 59.2–85.2 mn/lot, bids — zero. This is the most reliably established fact of the research and a direct indicator of the absence of developer demand.
- **Diagnosis of the failure — the economics of a "blank field."** The dominant version (3 of 4 streams): in addition to the land price, the developer is obliged to pay large **infrastructure contributions** (on the order of RUB 559–803 mn/lot, totaling >RUB 3.2 bn — *needs verification, single primary source*). Plus the macro backdrop working against launch: expensive mortgages after the cancellation of the subsidized program, an oversupplied NSO new-build market, a remote location without ready-made utility networks.
- **The tender failure pushed the delivery of the first homes to 2029; project completion — 2035.** The repeat tenders are postponed to summer 2026; the operator ARZhS is changing the scheme (it designs the homes itself, promises engineering readiness by 2027).
- **The demand anchor is real but narrow.** The driver is the SKIF synchrotron in Koltsovo (cost grew from RUB 37.1 to >50 bn, launch of the first phase pushed to 2025–2026). Koltsovo's science-town status has been extended to 2040 (which locks in federal funding). But the anchor demand from science is small (on the order of hundreds to a thousand jobs), and there is a risk that the district becomes a "dormitory" rather than a SmartCity.
- **Verdict on AI:** "AI INSIDE" the product is justified selectively (2 components + an auxiliary LLM layer), while ≈80% of the platform's value is classic, AI-free. The project's real pain is the developer-entry financial model and approvals, not a shortage of algorithms. Positioning it as an "AI city" = the "AI for the sake of AI" anti-pattern.

## Stage 1. The problem and the beneficiaries

### The problem (refined after the audit)
The framing "the city isn't being built yet" is imprecise. The project exists, has an operator, a master plan, federal backing (science-town status), an anchor (SKIF). The real problem: **a launched CRT (integrated territorial development) project is failing to attract developers** — the first tenders produced zero bids, housing delivery has slipped from "by 2025" to 2029. The bottleneck is the **financial model of developer entry**, not the absence of plans or political will.

### Who suffers / the beneficiaries
- **NSO government / JSC "ARZhS NSO"** — the client and risk-holder: funds were invested in infrastructure design (a RUB 652.3 mn contract), but attracting developers to the tenders failed.
- **Developers** — the target buyers of the CRT plots; they vote with their feet: 0 bids.
- **Residents/employers of the research-and-production cluster** (SKIF, Biotechnopark, the IT track, NSU) — potential anchor demand, but it is small.
- **Beneficiaries in case of success:** the science town of Koltsovo (~20.9 thousand residents, one of the few municipalities in the oblast with natural population growth) and NSO — a new research-and-residential district, integration into the transport corridor via the Eastern Bypass.

### The scale of the "frozen" capital
Already spent on engineering-infrastructure design (a RUB 652.3 mn contract); this is "frozen" capital with no construction. The tender failure pushes delivery of the first homes to 2029, with repeat tenders postponed to summer 2026.

| **"Akademgorodok 2.0" / "Naukopolis"** | Umbrella megaproject within which SmartCity formally sits | Federal status | Loss of momentum (of the many projects, mainly SKIF was funded); risk of "dormitory" development | The frame into which the project is embedded |

### SWOT of the SmartCity / Integrad project
- **S (strengths):** the science brand (Koltsovo, Akademgorodok, SKIF, Biotechnopark); a ready master plan (developer — "Commonwealth"); significant green zones (ecology); support from the NSO government; science-town status to 2040; future access to the Eastern Bypass (the "Russia" corridor).
- **W (weaknesses):** a "blank field," remoteness; incomplete transport connectivity (the bypass — 2026–2027); dependence on a single regional state operator (ARZhS) with no experience integrating science and housing; small anchor demand; high cost per sqm on the site.
- **O (opportunities):** federal money for SKIF/science; DOM.RF / SOPF infrastructure bonds as a mechanism for funding infrastructure instead of loading it onto the developer *(hypothesis — whether the project was submitted is not confirmed)*; INTC (innovation research-and-technology center) status with tax breaks *(hypothesis)*; an IT cluster.
- **T (threats):** a high key rate and the cancellation of the subsidized mortgage; oversupply in the NSO new-build market; competition from SKIF town for budget; the unresolved question of the land's category/ownership (forest fund / specially protected natural area); the risk of turning into a dormitory neighborhood.

- **TAM (hypothesis):** ~618 thousand sqm × price per sqm. At an NSO new-build sale price of ~RUB 161 thousand/sqm (May 2025), the upper revenue estimate over the horizon to 2035 is on the order of **~RUB 100 bn** (at ~RUB 120 thousand/sqm — ~RUB 74 bn). *The price per sqm must be verified.*
- **SAM (hypothesis):** the share actually sellable at current demand — estimated at 30–50% of TAM (limited by demographics and the rate).
- **SOM (hypothesis):** the next 3–5 years — de facto ~0 until demand resumes (tenders failed); with successful repeat tenders in 2026 — units of RUB bn.

### Monetization / city financing models
- **CRT with developer infrastructure contributions** (the current NSO model) — failed at the start: business will not take on the infrastructure burden in a greenfield.
- **DOM.RF / SOPF infrastructure bonds** — a concessional loan for engineering/transport/social infrastructure and preparing the territory for CRT. *Hypothesis: the key missing instrument — it needs checking whether the project was submitted.*
- **INTC** — a tax-break regime for the research-and-production zone. *Hypothesis, status not confirmed.*
- **PPP/concession** for engineering networks and social facilities.

## Stage 3. Technical expertise

### Technologies and land allocation
- Land allocation via the **CRT mechanism for an undeveloped territory** (Federal Law 494-FZ of 30.12.2020, Chapter 10 of the Town Planning Code): plots are carved out and sold to developers at tenders.
- **The category and ownership of the land under the CRT footprint are NOT CONFIRMED by any stream** (see "Open questions"). Indirect signals: part of the territory is forest/green zones, hence the "green frame" in the master plan; the historical eco-protest of 2021 (an indigenous birch grove, claims regarding the forest cadastre), because of which an investor withdrew its bid at the time. **This is potentially the largest hidden risk to the timeline** (reclassification of forest-fund lands, coordination with Rosleskhoz / possible lands of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education) — a hypothesis, needs verification against the cadastre.

### Norms
- **Federal Law 494-FZ / Chapter 10 of the Town Planning Code** — mandatory calculated indicators of the provision of utility, transport and social infrastructure and their accessibility (directly hits the project: social load and networks are an unresolved knot).
- **The national master-plan standard (VEB.RF, since 2021)** — advisory, does not replace the general plan/zoning / land-use permits / SP. Applicable: SP 42.13330, the NSO/Koltsovo general plan, zoning / land-use permits.
- **Koltsovo's science-town status extended to 2040** (resolution of the RF government, in force from 01.01.2026) — preserves federal funding for R&D and master plans.

### Transport frame — the Eastern Bypass
- Part of the "Russia" transport corridor (St. Petersburg–Vladivostok) and the M-52 "Chuysky Trakt" highway. Technical readiness ~75% (2025).
- The 1st stage (≈20 km) connects to the "Akademgorodok–Koltsovo" interchange; the first segment opened to traffic in 2025; **full commissioning of the 1st stage — 2026 (per some sources 2026–2027)**.
- **Conclusion:** without the bypass, the district's transport frame does not close and the Berdsk highway is not relieved. The connecting frame is physically being completed only by 2026–2027 — launching housing "along the highway" earlier is illogical. (The bypass length differs across sources: 80–83 km vs ~101 km — see "Contradictions".)

### Risks (technical plane)
- High cost per sqm on the site (per the ARZhS CEO — up to RUB 40 thousand/sqm versus RUB 11 thousand on the outskirts; **dubious**, not an independent calculation, the "RUB 11 thousand" figure is suspiciously low — see audit).
- Overload of existing local roads (Timakova, Ivanova, Lesosechnaya) and load on engineering networks without investment in adjacent districts.
- A weak operator: ARZhS has no experience of integrated development with a research-and-production component; critics compare the scheme with the "Klyukvenny" project (the risk of an "ordinary neighborhood").
- Multiplicity of master plans (several versions were commissioned) — a sign of an unsettled concept.

## Stage 4. AI APPLICABILITY (verdict by component)

### The distinction "built WITH AI" vs "AI INSIDE" (the key to an honest presale)
- **"Built WITH AI"** — Copilot/Claude/Cursor during development (generating GIS/CRUD boilerplate, dashboards). This is operational savings for the team; **it is not present in production**, and we do not sell it to the investor as a feature.
- **"Contains AI INSIDE"** — models actually working in the product (ML/optimization/LLM). This is exactly what is assessed below.

### Verdict table: AI INSIDE — YES / NO

| Platform component | AI INSIDE? | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Land/ownership registry, master-plan versions | **NO** | DBMS (PostgreSQL/PostGIS) + CRUD; AI is redundant |
| GIS / 3D zoning visualization | **NO** | Rendering and geoprocessing (MapLibre/Cesium/deck.gl) |
| Investor/government dashboard, tender/CRT statuses | **NO** | BI + business rules |
| Calculation of provision with social facilities | **NO** | Direct SP/SNiP norm — a deterministic formula, not a prediction; AI here is an anti-pattern |
| Lot financial model / infrastructure contributions | **NO** | Spreadsheet economics |
| Check for forest/protected area/encumbrances | **NO (at its core)** | Rules + PostGIS; ML only on a "dirty" cadastre |
| Transport modeling (forecast/calibration) | **YES (conditionally)** | ML on top of simulation given telemetry; measurable accuracy (interchange load, peak hour) |
| Multi-objective master-plan optimization | **YES — but NOT for the MVP** | Technically a "smart core," but it does not cure the cause of the tender failure (the master plan is already done by "Commonwealth"). Per the audit's recommendation — a second-phase differentiator |
| Demand/demographics forecast | **YES (weakly)** | On small data ≈ expert judgment; deploy cautiously |
| LLM assistant / RAG over norms and documents | **YES (auxiliary)** | A UX layer (semantic search over norms, auto-summary of versions); not the core of value, not to be used to justify the budget |

### Final verdict on AI
"AI INSIDE" is justified **selectively**: in transport modeling (given data) and as an optional LLM layer; multi-objective master-plan optimization — only as a second-phase differentiator, not the MVP. **The base product (≈80% of value and the entire MVP) is classic, AI-free.** The project's real pain is **financial-institutional** (the developer-entry model, infrastructure financing, holding a single concept together), not algorithmic. The platform is valuable primarily as a tool for transparency of tenders/CRT, calculation of contributions, and versioning of the master plan. Positioning it as an "AI city" is the "AI for the sake of AI" anti-pattern. (Precedents of AI in urban planning — Moscow's digital twin with AI, generative platforms — exist, but this is market context, not a justification for an AI core for this particular project.)

## Stage 5. Pitch

**Problem.** The launched CRT project "SmartCity/Integrad" (Koltsovo–Akademgorodok, ~600 thousand sqm of housing, by 2035) is failing to attract developers: the tenders produced zero bids. The economics of a "blank field" (high cost + large infrastructure contributions) and the macro backdrop (expensive mortgages, an oversupplied market) block developer entry. Delivery of the first homes has slipped to 2029.

**Solution.** An IT platform "digital master plan / SmartCity" for the operator (ARZhS) and the NSO government: transparency of tenders and CRT, calculation of infrastructure contributions and the lot financial model, versioning of the single master plan, an investor dashboard, control of provision with social facilities per the norms. Second-phase differentiators: transport modeling for the "Akademgorodok–Koltsovo" interchange and scenario optimization of development. AI — selectively, not as the core.

**Market.** First-phase clients: ARZhS NSO / Minstroy NSO (+ potentially SKIF town, Akademgorodok 2.0). More broadly — science towns / TOR / agglomerations of the RF. Caveat: DOM.RF is building a federal master-plan platform — position as niche modules/integrations rather than a head-on competitor. *(All market volumes are hypotheses, see stage 2.)*

**Tech.** Backend Python/FastAPI; data PostgreSQL + PostGIS; GIS/3D — MapLibre/Cesium/deck.gl; BI dashboards; integration with EISZhS/the cadastre. ML (transport, optimization) and LLM/RAG — as separate second-phase modules. Compliance with Federal Law 494-FZ, SP 42.13330, the VEB.RF national master-plan standard.

**Team.** *(Hypothesis — not part of the input data; to be filled in when preparing for the client.)* Needed: a GIS/urban-planning analyst, a backend engineer, a CRT/financial-modeling specialist, and for the second phase — an ML engineer.

**Roadmap.**
- **Q3–Q4 2026:** discovery with ARZhS/Minstroy NSO; verification of 3 blocking facts (land, contributions, who finances infrastructure in 2026); an AI-free MVP (registry, master-plan versions, tender dashboard, contribution calculation).
- **2027:** compliance with Federal Law 494-FZ norms, a CRT portal, integrations; a pilot for the "second attempt" tenders.
- **2028+:** AI modules (transport modeling given data, development optimization, LLM/RAG assistant) as a differentiator.

## Risks

1. **Terminological presale risk.** Pitching it as "why they aren't building a city" is naive. The correct framing: "why developer entry failed and how to unblock investment."
2. **The category/ownership of the land under the CRT is not confirmed** (forest fund? protected area? lands of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education? has the reclassification been done?) — a **blocking risk to the timeline**, a hypothesis.
3. **The financial model for a private developer does not work** — if the contribution burden stays on the developer, the 2026 repeat tenders risk failing again.
4. **Competition for budget** with SKIF town and Akademgorodok 2.0 (a possible cause of the outflow of federal money) — unproven by an official comparison of priorities.
5. **Demand risk:** the "housing ↔ jobs" balance is not calculated; with small anchor demand the district risks becoming a "dormitory."
6. **IT-product market risk:** the DOM.RF federal platform narrows the niche to integrations/niche modules.
7. **"AI for the sake of AI" risk:** the temptation to sell an "AI city"; the real pain is the financial model and approvals, not algorithms.
8. **Figures from media/presentations, not from a primary source** — see the list for mandatory verification below.

## Open questions / contradictions

### Open questions (not closed by any stream)
1. **The category and ownership of the land** under the CRT footprint: cadastral composition, presence of forest fund / protected area / Ministry of Science and Higher Education lands, whether the reclassification has been done, whether there are encumbrances. *(Question #1 — blocking.)*
2. **The reality and exact size of the infrastructure contributions** from the auction documentation (>RUB 3.2 bn — a single primary source, retold by three streams).
3. **Who finances the infrastructure in the 2026 "second attempt" scheme** — the NSO budget, the federation, ARZhS, or the developer again? The entire forecast for the repeat tenders depends on this.
4. **DOM.RF / SOPF infrastructure bonds** — was the project submitted? This may be the "missing link" of financing.
5. **INTC status** for the Koltsovo–Akademgorodok zone (tax breaks like Skolkovo's).
6. **The legal status of the "city"** — a separate municipality or a neighborhood within Koltsovo/Novosibirsk (affects budget, taxes, governance).
7. **The "housing ↔ jobs" balance** — how many jobs SKIF + Vector + Biotechnopark + NSU will provide by 2030 and whether they will be enough to absorb ~618 thousand sqm (~20 thousand residents).
8. **The "Klyukvenny" precedent** — how the same CRT scheme ended; a ready-made local case of failure.
9. **Competition with "SKIF town"** — do they compete for the same budget/demand and which is higher-priority for NSO.
10. **On the IT product:** will the region use the DOM.RF federal platform (which narrows the niche) or is there a mandate for its own solution; budget and procurement procedure.
