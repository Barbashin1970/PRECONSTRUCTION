# Preconstruction = Presale: one phase under different names

**Preconstruction** in construction and **pre-sales** in IT are
**the same project lifecycle phase**: research, design, estimation, and
planning **BEFORE implementation begins**. Both answer the question "is this worth doing, and how"
before the main money is spent.

> **Why this matters.** 70–80% of a project's success is determined in this phase. A high-quality
> presale yields −30% on budget overruns and −25% on delays. See
> [08 — economics](08-roles-economics-career.md).

## Analogy table

| Aspect | IT (Pre-Sales) | Construction (Pre-Construction) |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Understand requirements, propose a technical solution | Understand needs, create a construction plan |
| Feasibility | Feasibility Assessment | Feasibility Study |
| Context | Analysis of the client's IT infrastructure | Site inspection (terrain, soils, ecology) |
| Design | Solution Architecture (stack) | Design Development (drawings, engineering) |
| Regulation | Compliance (GDPR, infosec) | SP/GOST (Russian building codes), permits |
| Cost/timeline | Estimation (timeline) | Cost Estimation (budget estimate, schedule) |
| Team | Development staffing | Contractors, engineers |
| Risks | Technical, integration | Construction, natural |
| Concept | POC, demo | Visualization, mockups, 3D |
| Proposal | Commercial proposal, roadmap, budget | Tender documentation |
| Outcome | Contract → Delivery | Contract → Construction |

For an architect/urban planner this means: the IT presale methodology is easy to master by applying
the already familiar logic of construction design.

## Double presale in IT projects FOR construction

When we build an **IT product for construction** (a digital master plan, a GIS platform, a
regulations analyzer), the presale happens on two levels:

- **Level 1 — presale of the construction solution:** what domain problem are we solving?
  Who is the target audience (urban planners, developers, municipalities)? How does it fit into the construction cycle?
- **Level 2 — presale of the IT solution:** what architecture and technologies? And the key question —
  [is AI even needed in the product itself](04-ai-necessity-criteria.md)?

Both levels must work together: the product must solve a **real construction task
by technical means**.
