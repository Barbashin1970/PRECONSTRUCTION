# Why It Is Now Critically Important to Become a "Builder with AI-Research Skills" Rather Than an ML Engineer

## Key Thesis

**Don't try to become ML engineers for construction. Become builders with
AI-research skills for presales.** This is the path to competitiveness in the
labor market, high value to your employer, and the ability to influence projects with
maximum effectiveness.

---

## 1. Maximum Savings at the Preconstruction Stage: Avoiding Losses

**70–80% of a project's success is determined BEFORE construction begins.**

- 70–80% of a project's success depends on decisions made BEFORE construction begins.
- Projects with formal preconstruction processes have **30% less**
  budget overrun and **25% faster** delivery timelines.
- A well-executed preconstruction phase can deliver **30–40%** of potential savings.
- **69%** of all construction projects exceed their budget by more than 10%.
- **98%** of megaprojects are completed with delays or budget overruns.

### Where Money Is Lost: Preconstruction Error Statistics

The main causes of failure (which can be prevented with AI research):

1. **Delays** — weather conditions, material shortages, labor disputes.
2. **Change orders** — increases in cost and timelines.
3. **Design deficiencies** — incomplete drawings, inaccurate specifications.
4. **Financial risks** — fluctuations in material prices, inaccurate budget estimates.
5. **Operational risks** — unrealistic schedules, inefficient resource allocation.

### AI Research at the Presales Stage = the Main Tool for Savings

How AI helps avoid losses BEFORE construction:

- **Automated market analysis** — instant assessment of material prices and resource availability.
- **Parsing of regulations** — automatic extraction of requirements from SP, GOST, FZ (Russian building codes & federal laws).
- **Feasibility analysis** — rapid assessment of a project's technical feasibility.
- **Analysis of competitors and analogs** — finding successful cases and extracting lessons.
- **Risk analysis** — forecasting problems based on historical data.

**Example of savings:**

- Projects of $10M–$50M with proper preconstruction planning show an **ROI of 63%**
  on the investment in planning.
- Companies that spent just **1.5%** of their budget on preconstruction planning received
  **4%** profit (a ratio of 1 : 2.7).

> **Conclusion.** A specialist who masters AI tools for presales saves
> their employer millions of rubles by preventing errors at the planning stage.

## 2. Value to the Employer: New Roles and Competitiveness

New roles in construction with AI competencies (**not ML engineers!**):

1. **Construction Data Analyst** — analysis of AI-generated project data to
   improve planning, cost estimation, and resource allocation.
2. **AI Integration Specialist** — helping companies integrate AI into their workflows.
3. **AI-Powered Safety Officer** — AI safety-monitoring systems to reduce accident rates.
4. **BIM Data Analyst / Digital Twin Specialist** — working with AI-enhanced BIM and digital twins.
5. **AI Construction Project Manager** — managing projects with AI forecasting and optimization tools.

**Salary premium:** specialists with AI skills in construction earn
**25–40% more**. Demand for AI skills is growing **10% annually**.

> You don't need to become a programmer or an ML engineer. You need to learn how to
> use ready-made AI tools effectively to solve construction problems.

---

## 3. From Presales to Other Stages: Scaling Your Skills

Once you master AI research in presales, you become an expert in applying AI at ALL stages.

**Why presales is the ideal starting point:**

- It does not require deep ML/programming knowledge.
- Focus on prompt engineering and using ready-made tools.
- Quick results, applicable in real projects.
- Universal skills, transferable to any stage of the lifecycle.

**Application at other stages:**

- **Design:** automatic compliance checking against SP/GOST/FZ, clash detection
  in BIM, fire-safety analysis, compliance checking. Tools: CodeComply,
  Document Crunch, Civils.ai, Revit plugins.
- **Construction:** computer vision for PPE monitoring, detection of
  hazardous zones, predictive analytics for equipment. Case results: −95% accidents, −70%
  staffing costs, $1 million saved per prevented fatality.
  Tools: viAct.ai, DroneDeploy Safety AI, drones, Smart SafetyVest.
- **Voice input on the construction site:** Filio.io, AccuType, Benetics Voice AI, Kyro.ai,
  Sitevoice.ai. Saves 30–60 min/day on reporting, speech is 3× faster than typing, ROI 3.5×,
  −10+ hours of administrative workload per week per specialist.
- **Quality control (QC):** AI analysis of site photos, drones for hard-to-reach places,
  comparison of as-built reality with project documentation via computer vision.
- **Post-construction:** auto-generation of final reports, punch lists, operational documentation.

## 4. Why You Should NOT Become an ML Engineer

**Division of roles: creators vs. users of AI.**

| | ML engineer (creator of AI) | Builder with AI skills (user) |
|---|---|---|
| What they do | Develops models from scratch | Effectively applies ready-made tools |
| Knowledge | Mathematics, statistics, programming | Prompt engineering, AI capabilities/limitations |
| Where they work | IT companies that make AI products | Construction companies, at all project stages |
| Domain | Does not understand construction specifics | Deeply understands construction + knows how to apply AI |

> **Metaphor.** An ML engineer creates the hammer. A builder with AI skills masterfully
> uses the hammer to build a house.

**Labor market:** demand for ML engineers in construction is limited (only a few are needed);
demand for builders with AI skills is mass-scale (thousands are needed).

## 5. Career Development Strategy: the Path to Leadership

- **Stage 1.** Mastering AI research for presales (now, at the hackathon). Prompt engineering
  for business analysis, marketing, technical expertise; Perplexity Spaces, ChatGPT, Claude.
- **Stage 2.** Application in academic projects (6 months): analysis of urban-planning regulations, AI visualization
  (Midjourney, Stable Diffusion), voice notes → reports.
- **Stage 3.** Expansion to other stages (first year): CodeComply/Document Crunch, AI-BIM,
  voice assistants.
- **Stage 4.** Specialization (2–3 years): AI Safety Officer / BIM Data Analyst / AI Integration
  Specialist / Construction Data Analyst, certifications.
- **Stage 5.** Leadership (5+ years): Construction Tech & AI Lead, Digital Transformation Manager,
  Chief Innovation Officer.

**Why it is more effective than ML engineering:** 6–12 months of training instead of 3–5 years; immediate
applicability; the unique combination of "construction expertise + AI"; broad demand; a path into top management.

## 6. Competitiveness: Why Employers Will Choose You

An ordinary graduate: design, urban planning, regulations, AutoCAD/Revit/ArchiCAD —
the standard set. **You — a graduate with AI presales skills:** all of the same PLUS

- a feasibility study of a project with AI in 2–3 hours (others take 2–3 weeks);
- prevention of 30–40% cost overrun through high-quality presales analysis;
- automation of routine work (reports, code checking, documentation);
- understanding of modern technologies (vibe coding, AI integrations).

**In an interview, say** not "I can use Revit and I know the codes," but: "I know how to use
AI for presales analysis of projects, which saves the company up to 40% of the budget through
the prevention of errors at the planning stage; I am proficient with tools for automating
reporting and regulatory compliance checking, accelerating design by 30–50%."

**Portfolio cases:**

1. "Presales analysis of a residential complex" — Perplexity Spaces, analysis of market/competitors/codes
   in 3 hours, identified a 25% cost-overrun risk, saved 5 million rubles.
2. "Automation of SP compliance checking" — checking reduced from 2 weeks to 2 days, reduced risk of fines.
3. "Voice documentation on the construction site" — administrative work reduced from 10 to 2 hours/week, +15% crew productivity.

## The Final Formula

> **Construction expertise × AI skills = Competitive advantage.**

Three reasons to focus on AI presales:

1. **Maximum savings for the company** — 70–80% of success = the preconstruction stage, where you are the expert.
2. **Universality** — once you master AI in presales, you apply it at all stages.

Start with presales. Expand to other stages. Become indispensable.
