# Roles, Economics, and Careers

The "why" context: why mastering [presales](02-presale-5-stages.md) pays off more than
ML engineering.

## The Economics of Preconstruction (why this matters at all)

- **70–80%** of a project's success is determined BEFORE construction begins.
- Formal preconstruction: **−30%** budget overrun, **−25%** schedule.
- A high-quality phase → **30–40%** of potential savings.
- **69%** of projects exceed their budget by 10%+; **98%** of megaprojects run with delays/overruns.
- Planning ROI: projects of $10–50M → **63%**; spending 1.5% of the budget on planning → 4% profit (1 : 2.7).

The main preventable causes of failure: delays, change orders, design deficiencies, and
financial and operational risks. AI research at the presales stage tackles all five.

## A Builder with AI Skills ≠ an ML Engineer

| | ML Engineer (AI creator) | Builder with AI skills (user) |
|---|---|---|
| Does | Models from scratch | Applies ready-made tools |
| Knowledge | Math, statistics, code | Prompt engineering, AI capabilities/limits |
| Domain | Doesn't know construction | Knows construction deeply + AI |
| Demand | A handful | Thousands (mass-market) |
| Training | 3–5 years | 6–12 months |

> Metaphor: the ML engineer makes the hammer; the builder with AI skills builds a house with it.

## New Roles with AI Competencies

Construction Data Analyst · AI Integration Specialist · AI-Powered Safety Officer ·
BIM Data Analyst / Digital Twin Specialist · AI Construction Project Manager.

**Salary premium: +25–40%.** Demand for AI skills is growing ~10%/year.

## Career Path (from presales to leadership)

1. Mastering AI research for presales (the start).
2. Learning projects (6 months): zoning regulations, AI visualization, voice reports.
3. Other stages (1st year): code compliance checks (CodeComply, Document Crunch), AI-BIM, voice assistants.
4. Specialization (2–3 years): Safety Officer / BIM Data Analyst / AI Integration / Data Analyst.
5. Leadership (5+ years): Construction Tech & AI Lead, Digital Transformation Manager, CIO.

## Scaling to the Construction Stages

Once a specialist has mastered AI at the presales stage, they apply it across all stages:
design (SP/GOST (Russian building codes & federal laws) compliance checks, clash detection),
construction (PPE monitoring with computer vision: −95% accidents, −70% staffing costs),
voice documentation (−10+ hours/week of administrative load, ROI 3.5×), quality control
(drones, photo analysis), post-construction (automated reports, punch lists).

> **Formula:** Construction expertise × AI skills = Competitive advantage.
