# The 5 Presale Stages

The core of the methodology. An idea passes through five stages sequentially; each is led by an
[agent role](03-ai-agent-roles.md) and relies on real data with sources. AI speeds up the
research stages by 10–20×, and the packaging stage by 2–3×.

```
1. Identification → 2. Business analysis → 3. Technical expertise → 4. AI applicability → 5. Packaging
   of the problem      market/SWOT            feasibility             is AI needed?         pitch
```

## Stage 1. Identifying the problem and the opportunity

**Questions:** What problem are we solving? Who suffers (target audience)? What is the scale (how
many people/organizations)? What are the losses in time and money? Root causes?
**Role:** Business analyst. **AI:** searching for statistics and trends, real cases.
**Outcome:** a formulated problem with loss figures.

## Stage 2. Business analysis

**Questions:** Who is already solving the problem (analogues, competitors)? A SWOT of the idea
against their backdrop? The size of the Russian market (TAM/SAM/SOM)? Who will pay? Monetization
models?
**Role:** Business analyst + Marketer. **AI:** market parsing, SWOT, financial models.
**Outcome:** an assessment of viability and market potential.

## Stage 3. Technical expertise

**Questions:** What technologies are needed (languages, frameworks, databases)? Ready-made
libraries/APIs? Regulatory constraints (SP/GOST/FZ — Russian building codes & federal laws)?
Development complexity? Technical risks?
**Role:** AI systems analyst. **AI:** analysis of regulations, stack selection, feasibility.
**Outcome:** technical feasibility, stack, risks.

## Stage 4. Analysis of AI applicability — the KEY stage

**Question:** Is AI needed **WITHIN THE PRODUCT ITSELF**? This is the most important and most often
skipped stage.
**Role:** AI systems analyst (critical thinking).
**Essence:** to honestly assess whether the task can be solved by classical methods; to distinguish
"built **WITH THE HELP OF** AI" vs "contains AI **INSIDE**". Full criteria —
[04 — AI necessity](04-ai-necessity-criteria.md).
**Outcome:** a justified YES/NO recommendation and in which components.

## Stage 5. Packaging into a pitch

**Questions:** Elevator pitch (30 sec)? Structure of a 5-minute presentation? Key slides and
figures? Justification of the decision before management?
**Role:** Pitch packager (based on stages 1–4). **AI:** generating the structure, texts, and
visualizations. Template and the mandatory "AI applicability" slide —
[05 — packaging](05-pitch-packaging.md).
**Outcome:** a finished pitch with a verdict on AI.

## Demonstration walkthrough (case)

"Digital platform for analyzing urban planning regulations": (1) urban planners' problems with
regulations and statistics → (2) analogues + SWOT + Russian market → (3) PDF parsing technologies,
domestic GIS, FZ-218 → (4) "parsing — with the help of AI once, the platform runs on an ordinary
database; AI inside only for intelligent search, optional" → (5) elevator pitch and structure.

> are parallelized across agent roles, followed by an audit and consolidation into a single report
> (stage 5).
