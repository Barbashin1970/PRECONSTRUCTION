# Packaging into a Pitch

The final [Stage 5](02-presale-5-stages.md). It turns the conclusions of Stages 1–4 into a convincing
presentation for stakeholders. Led by the [Pitch Packager](03-ai-agent-roles.md).

## Structure

The **Problem — Solution — Market — Tech — Team** framework + elevator pitch (30 sec) + roadmap.
A 5-minute presentation (5–7 slides): Problem → Solution → Why Us → **Technology (is AI needed
inside)** → Market and Monetization → Roadmap → (opt. Team).

## Pitch Template

```
[PROBLEM]
Who: [target audience]
Suffers from: [problem description]
Scale: [figures — how many people / how much time / money]

[SOLUTION]
We offer: [the essence in 1 sentence]
How it works: [brief description]
Advantage: [why it's better than alternatives]

[TECHNOLOGY]
Stack: [technologies]
CRITICALLY: AI in the product — [YES / NO + justification]
  If YES: exactly where AI is used and why it is necessary
  If NO: how it is solved without AI and why this is optimal

[MARKET]
Size: [TAM / SAM]
Monetization: [business model]
Competitors: [how we differ]

[ROADMAP]
Step 1: [first action]
Step 2: [second action]
Step 3: [third action]
```

## Mandatory "AI Applicability" Slide

At the pitch — a dedicated minute and a dedicated slide: **is AI needed IN the product itself and why**.
Based on the [AI necessity criteria](04-ai-necessity-criteria.md). This is what is
evaluated most strictly: the honesty and soundness of the decision matter more than the "coolness of the AI".

## Packaging Tools

Gamma.app (generating presentations from text), Canva (templates), Google Slides + ChatGPT,
GigaChat (a 10–12 slide presentation). More details — [06](06-ai-research-tools.md).

## Pitch Quality Criteria

1. Depth of elaboration (25%) — how well all the stages were carried out.
2. Quality of AI tool usage (20%).
3. **Critical thinking (20%)** — honesty in assessing the necessity of AI.
4. Presentation and delivery (20%).
5. Practical value of the idea (15%).
