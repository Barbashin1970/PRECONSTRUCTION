# Preconstruction — knowledge base

The methodology **"Product management (presale) of construction projects with AI assistants"**:
how to use AI at the **research stage** of a project (preconstruction / presale) —
BEFORE implementation begins, where 70–80% of the cost of mistakes is saved.

> **Central principle.** AI is a powerful tool for **research and packaging an idea**, not
> a mandatory component of every product. We keep a separate distinction: "built **WITH THE HELP**
> of AI" (vibe-coding) ≠ "contains AI **INSIDE**" (ML in production).
